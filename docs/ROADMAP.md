# E-Franchise Roadmap

## Pay at Store

**Status: Planned — not implemented.**

Let a franchise partner choose **Pay at Store** for an eligible franchise-pickup order and pay at the assigned outlet instead of topping up the purchase wallet first.

- Offer Pay at Store in checkout and the insufficient-wallet popup, subject to admin eligibility settings.
- Show the amount due and pickup outlet before confirmation; label the order **Awaiting store payment**.
- Let authorized outlet POS staff record and verify cash, UPI, or card payment with a receipt/reference.
- Confirm payment once, prevent duplicate collection, and avoid debiting the purchase wallet for the same order.
- Release goods only after payment is verified and normal packing/guard checks are complete.
- Define stock reservation expiry, unpaid-order cancellation, refunds, and settlement reporting before rollout.

This is a separate payment method from franchise pickup and the local demo wallet top-up.
